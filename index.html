Exchange for Impact

Experience India. Unlock New Opportunities.

Pilot program in Delhi & Bangalore: 5 – 11 February 2027

‘Exchange for Impact’ aims to enable global leaders to assess India’s strategic relevance for their portfolios and define how businesses can participate in the growth story for India.

WHAT are the key agenda points?

Indian Market and Ecosystem

  • Macroeconomic insights
  • “Made in India” company visit and incubation centers
  • Insights through Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2027

Cross-cultural experiences

  • Dialogue on ‘How to do Business in India’
  • Cultural immersion and local leadership exchange

Digital India

  • AI innovation and digital perspectives
  • India’s Digital Stack and scaling models

Take-aways and Learnings

  • Structured reflections on key learnings
  • Business opportunities and follow-up actions
